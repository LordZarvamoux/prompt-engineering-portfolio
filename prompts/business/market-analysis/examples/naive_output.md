# Prompt Used

> Who is likely to buy a product like this: DeskIntelligence, a desk that adapts to what the consumer is doing. Ambient lighting for work and study, a distraction-free mode for tasks, and integrated AI for organizing products.

# Raw Output

People who are likely to buy DeskIntelligence include:

* **Remote workers and professionals:** They spend long hours at a desk and need features like adaptive lighting and distraction-free mode to stay focused and reduce eye strain during work hours.
* **Students:** High school, college, and graduate students who need a structured study environment can benefit from the task modes and AI organization to manage assignments and study materials.
* **Tech enthusiasts:** Early adopters who enjoy smart home technology and cutting-edge productivity gadgets will be drawn to the integrated AI features.
* **Content creators and freelancers:** Gamers, streamers, writers, and designers who need versatile lighting and organizational tools for dynamic work setups.

Overall, the primary market consists of individuals willing to invest in premium workspace technology to boost productivity, focus, and organization.
