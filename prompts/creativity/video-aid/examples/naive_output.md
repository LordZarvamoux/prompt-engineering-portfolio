# Prompt Used

> How could I edit a YouTube video around a story like this: What if erased from society? Someone from the year 2025 time travels over a millennium into the future in 3050. He sees two individuals talking to each other at an event and engages in a conversation. Eventually, the 2025 individual mentions a strange concept known as: “black people.” The two 31st century individuals laugh in mockery at how primitive the 21st century was after the 2025 individual explained the concept of race to them. “Sillest rubbish I’ve ever heard,” one of the of 31st century individuals said.

# Raw Output

Here is how you can edit a YouTube video around this story concept:

### 1. Pacing & Visual Editing
* **Cold Open:** Start *in media res* (right in the middle of the scene) with a quick 5-to-10-second preview of the 31st-century individuals laughing, followed by a fast smash cut to your title card.
* **Establishing the Setting:** Use fast cuts of futuristic visuals (cyberpunk/clean sci-fi B-roll) paired with ambient futuristic sound design (subtle hums, neon synth pads) to quickly build the year 3050 atmosphere without long, slow intro shots.
* **Visual Contrast:** Color grade the 2025 flashback or character elements in warm, modern tones, while making the 3050 scenes cool, sleek, and high-contrast to visually reinforce the 1,000-year gap.

### 2. Audio & Timing
* **Audio Cuts (J-Cuts & L-Cuts):** Let the sound of the 31st-century dialogue or laughter start a fraction of a second before cutting to their faces. This keeps the pacing punchy and dynamic for YouTube viewers.
* **The Punchline Moment:** Cut all background music completely when the 31st-century character says, "Silliest rubbish I've ever heard." Leaving 1–2 seconds of dead silence highlights the culture shock and character isolation before resuming the score.

### 3. Graphics & On-Screen Elements
* **Subtitles:** Use dynamic, pop-in captions for key dialogue to keep viewer retention high, especially during the conversation at the event.
* **Minimalist UI Elements:** Add subtle futuristic visual overlays (like holographic text or light HUD elements) to frame the 3050 setting without cluttering the screen.
