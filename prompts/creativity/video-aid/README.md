# Content Creation Aid | Cinematic Storytelling and Editing
> *The purpose of this prompt is to get personalized advice on how to improve your story and use editing techniques to tell that story in a visually cinematic way for the purpose of content creation.*

## Overview

This prompt produces advice on editing techniques that can be executed according to what the story can visually portray. This could solve a problem for people who are looking for ideas for the videos and how to edit them when they already have a concept for the video. People who could find this prompt useful may be content creators, or people who are not very experienced with editing. 

**Best for:**

- Longform content creators who want to develop a cinematic experience.

- Filmmakers who want to bring about a cinematic story in the best way they can.

- Editors who want to improve their editing skills.

**Structure:** C-A-R-E (Context, Action, Rules, Examples)

**Technique:** Few-shot

**Output:** A specific length for the prompt was not specified, but the format includes beginning with how to create an engaging and well-edited hook, as the hook is the most important part of a video. As for style, the LLM is expected to refrain from jargon without leaving footnotes defining them. 
---
## Quick Start

1. Open [`prompt.md`](./prompt.md) and copy the template.
2. Replace the placeholders:
- `[STORY SUMMARY]`: A summary of what your story concept/video idea is about. This is meant to provide the LLM context for what the story is about, and how to use that story to find the right editing techniques based upon the contents of that story.
3. Paste it into your AI model of choice and run it.
4. Review the output and adapt it to what you need.

## Customization Tips

- **Want more detail?** Specify to make this a step-by-step process throughout every single editing aspect of the story instead of a general overview. 

- **Want it shorter?** Set a maximum word-count limit. 

- **Different context?** Prioritize requesting video resources that meet your specific needs as opposed to just learning by text.
